## Ticket Authoring Rules

Every ticket carries all fields required by master directive §54. Tickets are sized for clear
ownership, clear review, clear testing, independent merge and parallel execution.

Not this: *"Build the entire dispatch system."*
This: a vertical slice that one owner can build, one reviewer can review and one QA engineer
can verify.

Equally, do not fragment work into microscopic tickets where coordination costs exceed
implementation costs.
