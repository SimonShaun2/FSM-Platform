## Objective
Establish the Architecture Decision Record process and record the decisions already made by
the restart, so architectural reasoning is preserved rather than relitigated.

## Product context
Master directive §39 requires stack changes to proceed only through an ADR, and §52 places
architecture decisions under `docs/adr/`. Several consequential decisions have already been
made — restarting on the existing repository, retiring the prior operating model, adopting
modular architecture over microservices. Two more are imminent and blocking: the tenant model
decision from FSM-0011 and the Stripe Connect charge model gating Sprint 8.

## User story
As an engineer encountering an architectural constraint, I need to find the reasoning behind
it, so I do not either violate it unknowingly or waste a sprint re-arguing a settled question.

## Scope
The ADR process, its template, and the initial decision records.

## Deliverables
1. `docs/adr/README.md` — the process: when an ADR is required, who authors it, who approves
   it, lifecycle states (Proposed / Accepted / Superseded / Deprecated), numbering, and how
   superseding works.
2. `docs/adr/ADR-TEMPLATE.md` — implementing master directive §39: problem, current approach,
   proposed approach, benefit, migration cost, operational impact, security impact,
   alternatives considered, decision, consequences.
3. `docs/adr/ADR-0001-restart-on-existing-repository.md` — **Accepted.** Records the decision
   to restart on `SimonShaun2/Renvani` (formerly `FSM-Platform`) preserving history rather than starting fresh;
   the mandatory Sprint 0 audit; the retirement of the four-agent model and the "thinner
   system" boundary; adoption of "Complete when needed, simple by default."
4. `docs/adr/ADR-0002-tenant-model.md` — **Proposed.** Placeholder framing the question,
   owned by TL-01, to be completed by FSM-0011. Blocks Sprint 2.
5. `docs/adr/ADR-0003-stripe-connect-charge-model.md` — **Proposed.** Frames direct charges
   versus destination charges versus separate charges and transfers, against merchant of
   record structure, platform liability, dispute and refund responsibility, platform
   economics, connected account experience and tax/reporting implications. Owned by BE-02 with
   TL-01. **Blocks Sprint 8** and is flagged in `PROJECT_STATE.md` §6 as requiring founder
   and possibly legal input.
6. `docs/adr/ADR-0004-modular-architecture.md` — **Accepted.** Records modular architecture
   over microservices per master directive §60, with the criteria that would justify splitting
   infrastructure later.
7. ADR index in `docs/adr/README.md`, maintained as records are added.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Template contains every field from master directive §39
- [ ] ADR-0001 and ADR-0004 complete and marked Accepted

- [ ] Index present and accurate

## Required tests
None. FSM-0010 should add a CI check that no ADR remains Proposed past the sprint it blocks.

## Impact
**API / Database / Frontend / Mobile / Integration:** none.

## Security considerations
ADR-0002 has direct security consequences — the tenant model determines every isolation
guarantee. QA-02 is a required reviewer when it moves to Accepted.

## Tenant isolation considerations
ADR-0002 is the tenant isolation decision. It must not be marked Accepted without FSM-0011
evidence and FSM-0009 adversarial findings.

## Observability requirements
None.

## Migration requirements
ADR-0002's outcome may require substantial data migration; that migration is planned in
FSM-0012's `MIGRATION_PLAN.md`.

## Rollback considerations
ADRs are superseded, never deleted. A reversed decision gets a new ADR marking the prior one
Superseded.

## Non-goals
Not deciding ADR-0002 — that requires FSM-0011. Not deciding ADR-0003 — that requires founder
input. Not retroactively documenting every V1 decision; only those that remain binding.

## Required review
TL-01 implements · Claude reviews ADR-0001 for product-statement accuracy · QA-02 reviews the
ADR-0002 framing.

## Required QA
QA-01 verifies the template covers §39 in full and that no ADR is Accepted without a decision
and consequences recorded.

## Completion procedure
Merge → ADR-0002 assigned to FSM-0011 → ADR-0003 raised with the founder as a Sprint 8 blocker
→ update `PROJECT_STATE.md` §5 and §6.
