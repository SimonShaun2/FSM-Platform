# Renvani company identity

## Company

Renvani helps home service businesses attract more customers through their own channels, turn more inquiries into booked jobs, and bring customers back. The platform connects growth and service operations so owners can build a stronger business and rely less on rented leads.

## North star

Help home service businesses own their growth: win more direct customers, deliver great service, and earn more repeat revenue.

## Identity

Renvani is a growth company for home services. AI powers how the product works; the promise is helping businesses grow and own their customer relationships.

## Product principle

AI agents describe how the product operates, not what Renvani is. Build growth and service capabilities that support direct customer acquisition, booked jobs, great service, and repeat revenue. Use Owner.com as a product and growth reference adapted to home services; do not pursue ServiceTitan feature parity.

## Product portfolio — home services

These are Renvani’s product areas, connected across one customer and service lifecycle. They are not separate apps. The areas organize the outcomes; the agents are how the work gets done.

1. **Growth:** Helps home service businesses attract local customers through their own branded channels and bring more direct inquiries into the business.
2. **Revenue:** Helps businesses respond to inquiries, qualify opportunities, prepare estimates, follow up, and convert more prospects into booked jobs. **Agent:** Revenue Agent.
3. **Operations:** Connects bookings to capacity and service delivery, helping schedule work, prepare crews, support job execution, and surface exceptions. **Agents:** Scheduling Agent and Service Agent.
4. **Retention:** Helps businesses bring customers back through recurring service and consent-based reminders, win-back, reviews, and referrals. **Agent:** Retention Agent.
5. **Command Center:** Gives owners visibility into outcomes, recommendations, approvals, and exceptions across Renvani. **Agent:** Owner Agent.

Growth includes branded acquisition and local discovery; the Revenue Agent handles inquiry conversion. Command Center is the owner-facing control experience across the product areas, not a separate specialist agent. Together, the portfolio supports direct acquisition → booked work → reliable service → repeat business.
